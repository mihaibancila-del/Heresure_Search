Something has to poll this schedule — the app cannot start itself on a timer. Locally that is the scheduler service (docker compose up -d scheduler, polls every IMPORT_POLL_SECONDS, default 60s); on the server it is the agent-licence-parser.timer systemd unit, polling every 15 minutes.
